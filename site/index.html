Five things, done well

No CRM, no ad manager, no website builder. Just what you need to write to your list, plus a command line for your agent.

  • Email designer

    Drag blocks into place, or paste in your own HTML and watch the preview change as you edit.

  • Contacts and tags

    Everyone in one list, sorted with tags. Import a CSV or a Mailchimp export, or add people one at a time.

  • Campaigns

    Send yourself a test and look it over. Nothing goes to your list until you say go.

  • Signup forms

    Paste a snippet onto your site. New subscribers confirm by email before they join your list.

  • Reports

    See how each campaign did: deliveries, opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes.

  • A CLI for your agent

    Everything in the app has a command, so Claude, Codex or any other agent can build and send for you.

We keep email wanted, too. Signup forms ask new subscribers to confirm, every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and we pause accounts whose bounces or complaints climb, so one sender can't hurt everyone else's mail. The details are in our anti-spam policy.
