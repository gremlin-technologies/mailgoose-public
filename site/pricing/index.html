Pricing

Start free. When your list outgrows it, the paid plan is $5 a month, and you buy credits for the email you send beyond your monthly allowance. Credits you buy last two years.

How credits work

  • Only campaigns use them

    Each campaign uses one credit per person it goes to. Test sends and the confirmation emails your signup forms send are free.

  • You see the cost first

    Before a campaign goes out, mailgoose shows how many credits it will use, and you confirm it.

  • Your monthly emails go first

    Every account gets 2,500 emails a month. A campaign uses those before any credits you bought. They don't roll over to the next month.

  • Bought credits last two years

    Credits you buy last 24 months from the day you buy them, and the oldest are used first. Cancelling the paid plan doesn't take them away.

When and how to buy

Buy credits when your monthly emails won't cover a campaign. Pay by card through Stripe; sales tax is added where it applies.

  2. Start the paid plan, monthly or yearly.
  3. Pick a pack and pay. The credits are there as soon as the payment goes through.

Questions

What counts as a contact?
Everyone on your list who's subscribed, or who signed up and hasn't confirmed yet. People who unsubscribed, bounced or marked you as spam don't count.
What happens when my list grows on the paid plan?
Your price goes up by $5 a month ($50 a year) for each further 10,000 contacts, from your next renewal. Nothing is charged partway through a month or year.
What if my free account goes over 1,000 contacts?
Your signup forms keep taking up to 100 more, so nobody who signs up is turned away, but your campaigns wait until you're back at 1,000 contacts or start the paid plan.
Can I cancel?
Yes, any time. The plan runs to the end of the month or year you paid for, and then your account goes back to free. Credits you bought stay, and still last 24 months from when you bought them.
Do prices include tax?
No. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies, worked out from your billing address.
