Where the others may suit you better

mailgoose is small on purpose. If you need any of these, one of the others is probably the better pick.

  • A bigger free plan

    Kit is free up to 10,000 subscribers, with its branding on your emails. Our free plan stops at 1,000 contacts.

  • Very large lists

    Brevo charges by emails sent rather than contacts kept. At 25,000 contacts and up it costs less than we do, as the table shows.

  • Automations, landing pages and SMS

    mailgoose sends campaigns and runs signup forms. The others add automated sequences, landing pages, SMS and more.

  • A team

    A mailgoose account has one person. The others let several people share one account.

Where these come from

We read each company's own pages on November 23, 2026. Prices and plans change, so check their sites before you decide. Spot something out of date? Tell us and we'll fix it.

  2. Mailchimp's help article on its plans: Free, Essentials, Standard and Premium, with features added at each step. It doesn't say on which plan the Mailchimp logo comes off, and doesn't mention a command-line tool or custom HTML. mailchimp.com/help/about-mailchimp-pricing-plans
  3. Mailchimp's help article on its Pay As You Go plan: credits expire 12 months after you buy them. mailchimp.com/help/about-the-pay-as-you-go-plan
