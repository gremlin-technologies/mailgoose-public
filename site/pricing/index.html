How credits work

  • Only campaigns use them

    A campaign uses one credit for each person it goes to. Test sends and the confirmation emails from your signup forms are free.

  • You see the cost first

    Before a campaign goes out, mailgoose tells you how many credits it'll use and waits for you to confirm.

  • Your monthly emails go first

    Every account gets 2,500 emails a month, and campaigns use those before any credits you've bought. Unused ones don't carry over to next month.

  • Bought credits last two years

    Credits you buy are good for 24 months from the day you buy them, and the oldest go first. They stay yours even if you cancel the paid plan.

When and how to buy

Buy a pack when your monthly emails won't cover a campaign. You pay by card through Stripe, with sales tax added where it applies.

In the web app

  1. Open Credits and billing.
  2. Start the paid plan, monthly or yearly.
  3. Pick a pack and pay. Your credits show up as soon as the payment goes through.

Questions

What counts as a contact?
Anyone on your list who's subscribed, plus people who signed up and haven't confirmed yet. People who unsubscribed, bounced or marked you as spam don't count.
What happens when my list grows on the paid plan?
Your price goes up $5 a month ($50 a year) for each further 10,000 contacts, starting at your next renewal. We never charge you partway through a month or year.
What if my free account goes over 1,000 contacts?
Your signup forms keep taking up to 100 more people, so nobody gets turned away, but your campaigns wait until you're back down to 1,000 contacts or you start the paid plan.
Can I cancel?
Yes, whenever you like. The plan runs to the end of the month or year you paid for, then your account goes back to free. Credits you bought stay, and still last 24 months from when you bought them.
How does this compare with Mailchimp and the others?
For a small list it usually costs a good deal less. We put mailgoose next to Mailchimp, Brevo, MailerLite and Kit, with links to where we read their prices, on how we compare.
Do prices include tax?
No. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies, based on your billing address.
